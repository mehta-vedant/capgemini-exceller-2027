# Candidate experience evidence

Status: research placeholder for an expanding local source registry.

No candidate anecdote is treated as official assessment policy. Any future
experience report must retain its URL, capture date, assessment year, and an
`CANDIDATE_REPORTED` label. It must not be turned into a claimed exam question.

The product's first-release exercises are original, syllabus-aligned practice;
they are not recalled, leaked, or copied assessment content.
