# Research gaps and boundaries

- Employer materials confirm assessment headings and some durations, not a
  public item bank or scoring rubric.
- Cognitive Motion and Grid challenges are not recreated.
- Candidate reports, if added, remain unverified and must be labelled.
- The 280-item bank is an initial quality threshold, not a claim of complete
  coverage or a 1,160-item bank.
- Gemini and Docker are optional local dependencies; their unavailable states
  are shown honestly rather than simulated.
